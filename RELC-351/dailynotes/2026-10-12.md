## Worship

### Liquid sacrifice

- *Liquid* sacrifice (liquid libation). 
  - Priest mixes a hallucinogen with milk and drinks it.
  - Thing of the past, as many countries have passed laws forbidding hallucinogen use in religion.
    - The LDS church has contributed to protecting this practice in lawsuits. That's neat.

### Marriage

- Marriage & child-bearing is commanded.
- Ritual washing & prayers before & after intimacy.
- Marriage rituals: 
  - Sheet is held between man & wife until they're pronounced husband & wife. Then it drops, which is the cue for both to thrwo rice at each other.
  <!-- - Something about showering down all your needs and wants on each other. -->
    - "Throughout our married life, I promise to shower you with blessings & needs & wants" (symbolized by showering each other w/ rice).
  - Something about a cord tying their hands together, similar to Hinduism.

### Sacred clothing

- *Sudreh* ("undershit") w/ bag/purse of righteousness.
  - Worn the rest of your life, every day (except if you're swimming, showering, etc.).
  - Metaphorically, you're supposed to fill the purse of righeousness w/ good thoughts, words, deeds.
- *Kusti* ("belt").
  - Woven out of 72 threads of wool (one of the Zoroastrian books of scripture has 72 chapters).
  - Wrapped around you three times: Once for good thoughts, once for good words, and once for good deeds.
  - Reminds you to suppress/control your sexual desires.
- Not as "close to the vest" about these as LDSaints would be about garments.
  - e.g., a Zoroastrian guy in Gaskill's class opened his buttoned shirt to show his *Sudreh*. 
- Built into the day: 7 different times to stop and assess if you're living as the garment teaches you to live.

### Incense

- Believed the smell of Frankensince is what it smells like in God's presence.

### Buildings

- Known as "fire temples."
- In India, only Zorastrians are allowed inside. In the West, they generally let in anyone w/ good intentions.
- Each temple features a flame that is constantly burning. Flames symbolize God/God's power.
  - There is one in Iran that has a fire that has been burning since 453 BCE.
- Different level of fire temples.
  - Highest level: very specific about what wood can be used, etc. Only 9 of these exist in the world today.

### Prayer

- Most important liturgical rite.
  - They believe nothing keeps the devil at bay better than prayer.
    - You can't talk to God & the devil at the same time.
- Wash your hands & face before prayer (symbolically).
- Traditional Zorastrians believe you have to pray 5 times a day.
  - During these, you would untie your *Kusti* and evaluate if you've been living up to what it represents.
  - Very similar to Islam: You cite passages of scripture or smth idk.
- Outside of those 5-times-a-day prayers, you're allowed to pray from your heart.

### Fasting

- Strong view on fasting: *Don't* do it.
  - Zarathustra explained: We fast from committing sin.
  - This is in contrast to Isaiah, who taught we fast to avoid sin. Zarathustra is basically saying, "If you're fasting to avoid sin, why not just... avoid sin?" 
    - (Isaiah 58: "Is not this the fast I have chosen? To loose the bands of wickedness? That ye make break every yolk?")

## Symbols

### Fire

- You can have an in-dwelling of one of the six major divinations of God.
- Have been accused (esp by Christians) as fire worshippers. That's nonsense. Fire is a symbol of God. They don't believe fire is god, but they treat it respect b/c of what it represents.


### Faravahar

- The ultimate symbol of Zoroastrianism.
- This is that big wingspan with like the three layers of feathers (good thoughts, words, deeds).
  - On the other hand, the feathers on the tail, being lower than God, are bad thoughts, words, deeds.
- Figure in the center is understood to be a representation of God.
  - Turning away from Angra Mainyu, towards Spenta Mainyu.

## Basic beliefs

### Authority

- Traditionally **patrilineal**&mdash;authority is passed from father to son.
- Priests' responsibility is to tend the sacred fire.
  - When functioning in preisthood capactiy will wear white robe, sash, and cap. (White being the color of purity 'n shih.)
    - If you're one of the priests attending the literal fire, you wear a mask to protect the fire from your saliva, which is seen as impure.
- *Hamazor*: Each person offers their hand interlocked with their neighbor. When this is performed, personal differences are forgotten.

### Beliefs on Women

- Claims men & women are absolutely equal but absolutely different.
- Men & women have different (though allegedly equal) roles.
  - e.g., women don't get ordained.
    - There are currently on planet earth *six* female Zoroastrian women who have been ordained.
- Patriarchal society.
- Believe that when women are menstruating, they are under attack by the devil.
  - Thus, during her cycle, she doesn't sleep or have sex with her husband, she wears specific clothing, doesn't interact with priests or men functioning as priests, etc.

### The Devil

- One of God's offspring.
- Great battle between devil and God's good in the time before mortality.
- Zoroastrians believe that at some point, good god's armies will fight devil's armies, and the former will win.
- Devil's characteristics is very similar to what mormons teach about Lucifer:
  - Ignorant.
  - Totally evil.
  - Tries to turn us away from God.
  - Functions with permission of God. 

### Spirits

- Our spirits existed before mortality.

### Heaven

- Divided into strict degrees of glory. (Paradise, Sun, Moon, Stars, and Outer Darkness.)

### Contamination

- Soil, fire, and water are believed to be sacred and must not be contaminated.
- ^ For this reason, Zoroastrians have been very careful about disposing their waste.
  - e.g., using chemicals to dispose of your urine/waste or to spit in after brushing. Or perhaps using water that is guaranteed to not go back into the water cycle. etc.
- Traditionally, dead bodies could not be buried in the ground, dropped in the ocean, or burned in fire (because that would be unclean). So instead they would let vultures & other scavengers to eat your corpse, then your skeleton (after being left to dry for some time) would be taken to a pit, where your bones would (from natural means) turn to dust.
  - Only functioning of these today are in India.
  - Today, some Zoroastrians would use a "solar" oven.
  - Some modern (neo) Zoroastrians have been arguing for organ donation. The last good deed any of us do is feed animals with our corpse&mdash;but why not give our body as a donation? Isn't that just as good as feeding them to animals?

## Denominations 

- *Zero* off-shoots/denominations.
  - There have been riffs (e.g. neo-Zoroastrianism), but no fractures.

## Historical development

- 550-330 BCE: Spanned Asia, Africa, and Europe.
- Today: 112K worldwide.
  - Most live in Eastern Iran or Mumbai India.
  - Only 11K Zoroastrians in North America.
    - Most are in LA.
- Reasons for decline in popularity:
  - Low birth rates.
  - Doesn't accept converts.

But Zoroastrians believe that entry to heaven is based solely on your thoughts, words, & deeds, not your subscription to any religion. One of Gaskill's Zoroastrians friends believed that even if Zoroastrianism as a religion ceased to exist, the number of people that would gain entry to heaven would not decrease.
