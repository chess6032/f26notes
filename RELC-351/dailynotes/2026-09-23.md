### *Omer*

- Right after Passover.
- Somber holiday.
  - One of the things you observe during this holiday is a giant plague during 2nd century in the common era.
- Pseudo-celebratory thing: In Orthodoxy, you do not cut a boy's hair until the *Omer* after his second birthday.
