# Hinduism cont...

- Hinduism does not proselyte.
  - Hinduism believes you actually shouldn't convert from the religion you're born in. The religion you're born in is a product of your *karma* (i.e., you're only born into Hinduism if you've been good in your past lives) and your *dharma* (i.e., your path for this life was meant to be walked in the path of the religion you were born in.)
  - Hinduism does not have a given set of laws. Your law is your *dharma*.
    - e.g., if a Hindu saw an mormon drinking, they would encourage them to stop because they are not being true to their *dharma*.


## Karma

- Karma is seen as the natural justice that occurs as a result of your bad/evil actions.
  - More specifically, it builds up when you're not following your *dharma*.
- "Good karma" is a Western phrase. "Good karma" itself is an oxymoron. It's like saying "good evil."
- The trail of karma follows back all the way to the beginning of time. There is no beginning, only a plan for the end (Nirvana).

## Castes

- Caste system is very rigid. There is no mobility, and you're not allowed to marry between castes.
  - There are stories of a man and woman from different castes eloping so they can be together. Their families write, saying they're no longer mad. So the couple comes back... only for their families to stone them to death.
- Interaction between other castes is limited.
- Top three castes wear a *Janeu* after they come of age. 

## Gandhi

### Castes

Gandhi was celebrated (by non-Hindus) for his opposition to the caste system.

- Hindus would generally say Gandhi was trying to let mercy rob justice.
- Hindus&mdash;even those that generally liked his teachings&mdash;have a hard time understanding his opposition to the caste system. The caste you're born into isn't a result of bad luck, you were born into it because it's what you need for salvation.
- Gandhi himself was actually the 2nd-highest caste. But, to resonate more with all people, he fudged his caste and acted as the 3rd-highest (which has the most populous).
